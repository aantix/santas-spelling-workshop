🧝 Elf's Secret Code: Vowel Teams!
Two letters team up to make ONE sound.
🌙 oo says /oo/: moon, spool, cartoon
🥱 aw & au say /aw/: aw at the end or before n, l, k (yawn, hawk, crawl). au in the middle (causes, vault, haunted)
🪙 oi & oy say /oy/: oi in the middle (oil, coin, spoiled). oy at the END (destroy, enjoy, cowboy)
✏️ Dictation: My sister enjoys horses.
